        
## the problem

imported supplements come in a lot of brands, sizes and flavours. the same creatine in four flavours and two tub sizes is eight products. sold through messages, that's a lot of "do you have the mango one in the big tub?"

## what we built

a woocommerce store where the catalog does the answering.

- **a page for each brand:** wellcore, muscleblaze, anabolic, gold, gym leader and applied nutrition each have their own page, so people shop by the brand they trust.
- **a homepage that sorts the shelves:** best sellers, limited stock and per-brand rows, so what's popular and what's nearly gone is easy to spot.
- **wishlist and accounts:** customers can save products and come back to their orders.
- **a payment page for local methods:** customers pay in taka, and the order updates when they do.
