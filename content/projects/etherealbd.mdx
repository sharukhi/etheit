## what we built

a shopify store where the whole range is browsable and orderable without a conversation.

- **collections that match how people shop:** denim, formals, tops and bottoms, plus the brand's own drops, like legacy, player by chance, revolution and summer.
- **sale pricing that explains itself:** the old price sits next to the new one, in taka, so a discount reads as a discount.
- **a homepage that changes with the season:** the banner, the featured collections and the product rows can be swapped by the team without calling us.
- **checkout set up for bangladesh:** prices in BDT, and payment options that local customers actually use.
